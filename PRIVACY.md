# Privacy

PhoneBridge has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no telemetry. It never connects to the internet.

- **What moves:** notifications (app name, title and text), SMS you receive, and clipboard contents (text, links, images), only from your phone to your own Mac and back, over your local Wi-Fi, end-to-end encrypted.
- **What's stored:** the pairing key on both devices; on the phone, your Mac's local address and port, your on/off choices and today's counts (numbers only). Nothing else is kept. The last image copied from the Mac sits in the app's private cache so it can be pasted.
- **What's not collected:** nothing is sent to Harshkit or anyone else.
- **Your controls:** turn any feature off in the phone app, or quit the Mac app from its menu. Uninstall both apps and the data goes with them.
